Notice No. 3
on the suspension of sign-in to state services by Tulpar ID

Talas, 9 October 2026

1. In view of the notice of the Bank of Irad of 29 September 2026 and the instruction of the Department of Public Information on the Tulparcoin service, sign-in to e-Irad and other state services by Tulpar ID is suspended from 9 October 2026 pending a review of the service's compliance with the conditions for state services, including the storage of the personal data of residents of the ISEZ.

2. The suspension does not affect the use of the Tulpar application by residents, nor the Official channel of e-Irad on Tulpar.

3. Services that do not need sign-in remain available. Applications that need identification are completed at the counter with an ID card.

4. The date of resumption will be announced separately.
