The e-Irad card

The e-Irad card is a chip card that will let residents of the ISEZ sign in to e-Irad and sign applications electronically. It does not replace the ID card or the passport.

IssuedAfter the pilot. The date will be announced on e-Irad.
WherePublic Service Centres: Talas, Liberty Boulevard 14; Nurzhan, Azatlık Prospect 31; Iyık Janyl, City Hall.
FeeIS 150 (pensioners and persons with disabilities free). Card reader for home use IS 1,200 at Attelkom service points.
ValidFive years. The PIN is given in a sealed envelope; three wrong PINs block the card.
BringYour ID card or passport and proof of registered address.

Questions

Can I use my bank card instead?
No. Bank cards, including Talasbank cards, cannot be used to sign in to state services.

Will it work on a phone?
The card needs a card reader connected to a computer with Windows. A mobile version is under study.

Who can get one?
Citizens and residents of the ISEZ aged 16 and over with a registered address in the ISEZ.
